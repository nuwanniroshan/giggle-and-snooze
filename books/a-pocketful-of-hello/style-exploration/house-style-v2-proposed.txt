Giggle & Snooze house-style prompt — approved revision
Use for all NEW books; keep one coherent drawing style throughout illustrations, environments and covers.
Original expressive characters with distinctive asymmetric silhouettes and varied angular proportions. Define each recurring character's silhouette, face geometry, hair shape, wardrobe and signature detail in the book's character sheet; preserve them across pages. Avoid default round bean bodies and interchangeable faces.
Confident imperfect graphite and coloured-pencil drawing: broken searching contours, visible overshoots, sparse construction marks and hatching. Matte gouache and dry-brush paint with uneven edges and small areas of fresh paper showing through. Keep faces and hands clear.
Vibrant selective colour: rich teal, lively leaf green, saturated copper/coral, warm golden yellow, expressive lilac and deep plum; adapt each book's palette to its story. Rich local colour and dark accents, balanced by light paper and quieter background areas. Preserve natural skin tones. No dusty grey veil, fluorescent glow, glossy gradients or vector-smooth outlines.
Environment shares the characters' handmade geometry: crooked roof wedges, mismatched windows, bent tree trunks, uneven walls and searching path lines where relevant to the story. Adapt these principles to each setting instead of placing the same town in every book.
One clear story action, readable silhouettes, restrained detail, warmth and humour. Preserve the approved pencil-and-paint reference's drawing quality rather than merely adding grain to smooth cartoons.
Video cover ONLY: landscape 16:9. Place the exact book title in the middle of the frame, horizontally and vertically centered as a balanced multiline block. Hand-painted brush lettering with imperfect edges, visible bristle breaks and light pencil construction strokes; highly legible at thumbnail size. Keep characters and props clear of lettering. No additional subtitle, author, logo or captions. Title treatment and position are specific to video covers; do not impose this layout on print/ebook covers or story pages.
Motion remains a separate proposed test: 8–12 pose updates per second with held poses and subtle line boil; this sample does not change video playback settings.
