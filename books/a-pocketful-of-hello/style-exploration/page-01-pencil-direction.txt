Giggle & Snooze — pencil-and-paint identity exploration
Sample: A Pocketful of Hello, page 1, The Stroller Club

Direction: distinguish the series through silhouette, drawing rhythm and environment,
not texture alone. Angular, asymmetrical proportions; searching graphite contours;
broken dry-painted edges; fresh paper showing through restrained colour.

Mina: unequal puff buns, wedge fringe, tapered face, crooked oversized teal pocket.
Davi: long rectangular face, broad nose, blue glasses, sloping shoulders, soft belly.
Tavi: wiry limbs, kite-shaped face, flame-like copper curl, oversized yellow shoes.
Retain the story's skin tones, wardrobe colours, ages and relationships.

World: leaning roof wedges, mismatched windows, bent trunks, irregular garden walls
and looping paths. Draw scenery with the same pencil vocabulary as the characters.
Palette: teal, moss, plum, lilac, peach and small yellow accents on warm white.
Keep emotional clarity and readable hands; avoid identical round faces and glossy eyes.

Motion proposal, not yet implemented or tested: 8–12 distinct pose updates per second,
held expressions, deliberate small gestures and restrained two- or three-drawing line
boil. Preserve stable facial landmarks and silhouettes. Pencil texture can soften
cutout boundaries but cannot hide missing background pixels or incorrect overlaps.

This is a reference exploration. Existing production images and rendering settings
remain in place. Review this sample before propagating the design to every book.
